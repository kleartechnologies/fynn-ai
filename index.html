How it works

Four steps from nervous to ready.

Upload your resume. Practice realistic interviews. Receive AI coaching tied to exactly what you said. Track your readiness growth over time.

Step 01

Upload your resume.

Finn reads your experience, finds the skills that matter, and tailors every question to your target role.

Step 02

Practice realistic interviews.

Speak to Finn like a real interviewer. He listens, asks follow-ups, and pushes you when an answer is too vague.

Step 03

Receive AI coaching.

Every score is traceable to the moment in your answer that earned it. No black-box AI — just clear next steps.

Step 04

Track readiness growth.

Watch your readiness score, streaks, and session history build into a clear picture of how ready you really are.

Inside the app

Everything you need to walk in ready.

A complete coaching loop — built around the way real interviews actually feel.

Resume Analysis

Finn parses your resume into a structured profile — work history, skills, and gaps — then tailors every question to your background and target role.

AI Interview Practice

Choose a role, interview style, and difficulty. Finn runs a realistic mock interview — asking the kinds of questions and follow-ups a real interviewer would.

Real-Time Coaching

Get instant, evidence-based coaching after every answer — what worked, what didn't, and exactly what to say differently next time.

Readiness Score

A rolling 0–100 score across six signals — clarity, depth, evidence, structure, ownership, and delivery — so you always know where you stand.

Interview Replay

Review your full session afterward — transcript, scores, and coaching side by side — so you can see exactly how the interview unfolded.

Career Role Personalization

One profile, many ladders. Fynn adapts coaching, question style, and difficulty to the specific role and seniority you're targeting.
