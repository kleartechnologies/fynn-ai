Permanent action

Delete Your Account.

You can permanently delete your Fynn AI account and associated data at any time. This action is permanent and cannot be reversed.

What gets deleted

When you delete your account.

Everything tied to your Fynn AI account is removed from active systems and purged from backups within 30 days.

Account profile — name, email, password, sign-in method, and profile preferences are removed.

Resume uploads — original files and parsed structured data are deleted.

Interview history — every practice session, transcript, and feedback record is deleted.

Readiness scores — all stored signals, scores, and rolling readiness estimates are removed.

Progress data — streaks, weekly trends, milestones, and saved drills are deleted.

Subscription access — active subscriptions remain billed by your app store; cancel in your store's subscription settings before deleting.

Deleted data cannot be recovered. Make sure you've exported anything you want to keep before requesting deletion.

How to request

Three steps.

The quickest way is by email. Use the address you signed up with so we can verify the request.

  1. 1

    Cancel any active subscription.

    In the App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Subscriptions are billed by the store and don't end automatically when you delete your account.

  2. 2

    Email support@fynn-ai.com

    Subject line: Account Deletion Request. Send from the email tied to your Fynn AI account.

  3. 3

    We confirm and delete within 30 days.

    You'll get a confirmation email when deletion is complete. Active data is removed within 7 days; backups are purged within 30.
